How it works

The work is split so that each part does what it is good at. The AI reads messy documents; plain code does the checks that must be exact; the agent decides what to do next.

The AI reads

Documents in, facts out

Scanned or digital, Arabic or English, Hijri or Gregorian dates. Each PDF becomes the same short list of facts: company name, CR number, IBAN, expiry date.

Code checks

Rules that can't be talked around

Is anything expired or missing? Is the CR active in Wathq? Does the bank account belong to the same company? Is the IBAN valid? The rules live in one settings file.

The agent acts

Fix, finish, or stop

Routine problem: it emails the supplier for exactly the missing document. All clear: it creates the vendor. Risk: it stops and asks you, with the evidence.

It never changes bank details on its own. Any email asking to move a supplier's payments to a new account is frozen until a person calls the supplier on the phone number already on file. This is the most common way companies lose money to email fraud.
